Privacy and Cookie Policy

Information on the processing of personal data pursuant to EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR).

Data controller

Plutone — Via Montello, 7A - 31100 Treviso (TV), Italy — e-mail: plutone.treviso@gmail.com.

Data collected

We collect only the data you provide voluntarily through the forms on the site or by e-mail: name or company name, e-mail address, address of the property offered and the content of the message.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Replying to requests for information, for spaces or for the assessment of a property (pre-contractual measures, art. 6.1.b GDPR);
  • sending the newsletter with news on available spaces and initiatives in Treviso, only if you have given your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR). You can unsubscribe at any time.

Retention

Request data is kept for the time necessary to handle it and in any case no longer than 24 months after the last contact; newsletter data is kept until consent is withdrawn.

Your rights

You can at any time request access to, rectification, erasure or restriction of your data, object to the processing and withdraw your consent by writing to plutone.treviso@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Cookie Policy

This site does not use profiling cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may record the visitor's IP address in accordance with its own policy.

With your consent we use Microsoft Clarity, an analytics tool by Microsoft that collects anonymous data on how the site is used (clicks, scrolling, heatmaps and session recordings) to improve the experience. Clarity sets its own cookies and processes data under Microsoft's privacy statement. The tool starts only after you press "Accept" in the banner; you can decline with no consequence for using the site.
