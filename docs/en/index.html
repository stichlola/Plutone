The team

Who is behind Plutone

Giorgia

Corrado

Davide

We are Corrado, Giorgia and Davide. We believe every closed shutter is a wasted opportunity for the local area.

We created Plutone to solve two big problems: the emptiness of vacant shops and the difficulty of finding accessible physical spaces. A single solution designed to give value to the city, supporting both those looking for a place for their ideas and those who want to make the most of their property.
