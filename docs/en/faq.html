Help Centre

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do you have doubts about how the service works? We have gathered the answers to the most common questions from businesses and owners.

For Businesses and Brands

What is the minimum duration for renting a space?

No strict commitment. With Plutone you have total freedom of duration: you can rent a space for a day, a week, a month or as long as you prefer. Flexibility is the heart of our service.

Can I rent just a window?

Yes! You can choose to use only a window for exhibitions, displays or advertising material, cutting costs. It is a great alternative if you don't need the interior space, or to keep your visual presence in the centre even after a physical event has ended.

For Property Owners

What happens if I find a long-term tenant?

You stay completely free. Our contracts are all very short-term (from a few days to a few months). You just need to wait for the end of the short period already agreed — of which you will be informed in advance — and you will be free to let the property permanently. No commitment or penalty.

How are payments guaranteed?

With Plutone the risk of late payment is zero. Our contracts provide for advance payment and a deposit. If payment is not made, the temporary activity does not start.

General Information

Where does Plutone currently operate?

At the moment we operate only in the historic centre of Treviso, selecting properties and windows in the areas with the highest footfall in order to enhance the local urban fabric.
