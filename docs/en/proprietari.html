Our commitment to you

Your property always stays in excellent condition, ready to be let long-term whenever you wish. We prevent the deterioration and the extraordinary maintenance typical of spaces left closed for a long time.

For owners

The Benefits for your Property

01

Payment security and complementary income

With Plutone you run no risk of late payment: our contracts provide for advance payment and a deposit. You earn a complementary income.

02

No long-term commitment

You stay completely free. You can decide at any time to let your property long-term or use it for something else. No prolonged commitment or obligation towards us.

03

Visibility for your space

Those who try your space temporarily and succeed are very likely to turn their business into a stable lease. Besides, an active space is seen as more desirable.
